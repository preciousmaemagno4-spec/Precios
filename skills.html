Skills I'm Developing

As a student, Being a student is not just about earning high grades or finishing school requirements. It is a journey of growth, where I learn more than just lessons from textbooks—it is where I build the foundation for who I want to become in the future. As I pursue my studies, especially in the field of Information Technology, I am developing different kinds of skills that will help me succeed not only in school but also in my future career and daily life.

Information Technology

  • Computer Literacy — Understanding how technology works HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • File Management Organizing files and folders properly
  • Creating modern and accessible web interfaces

Graphic Design

  • Design Principles Balance, contrast, alignment, repetition, and hierarchy
  • Color Theory — Choosing harmonious colors, understanding moods, and accessibility
  • Layout & Composition — Organizing elements for clear, beautiful designs

Public Speaking

  • Confidence — Overcoming nervousness and speaking with self-assurance
  • Clear Communication — Expressing ideas simply so others can understand easily
  • Body Language — Using eye contact, gestures, and posture effectively
  • Active Listening — Understanding others before responding

Problem Solving and Critical Thinking

  • Identifying Problems — Understanding exactly what the issue is before trying to fix it
  • Thinking Critically — Looking at facts and details instead of guessing
  • Finding Solutions — Thinking of more than one way to solve a problem
